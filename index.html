ADHDers Club @ York University

A student-led space for support, awareness, and connection at York University.

What We Do

Destigmatize ADHD
Empower Students
Build Community

We raise awareness about ADHD and mental health at York University while creating an inclusive space for students to connect, share experiences, and find support. Through resources, workshops, and collaborations, we provide practical tools to help students better understand and manage ADHD. Stay tuned for our upcoming events!
